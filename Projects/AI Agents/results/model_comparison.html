Model Selector Rationale

Prophet outperforms SARIMA overall (MAPE 12.4% vs 14.8%) and on 2 of 3 segments. The 4-year series with strong, growing year-end seasonality and clear holiday effects (Black Friday / Christmas) favours Prophet's multiplicative seasonality and holiday calendar. SARIMA edges ahead on Furniture — a category with more erratic, less trend-driven demand — where a parsimonious ARIMA(1,1,1)(0,1,1)[12] (AIC 487.3) captures the pattern well. An ensemble is not recommended: the models disagree by an average of $4,200/month, which is meaningful, and Prophet's structural advantage is clear enough to commit to.
